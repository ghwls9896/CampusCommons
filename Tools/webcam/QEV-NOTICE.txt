qev_backend.predict_image adapts the OptionModel.encode inference sequence for
an in-memory PIL image and one fixed activity question. It reuses upstream
prompt packing, condition/readout heads and calibration; camera images never
pass through upstream file-based image loading. Model weights are unmodified.
The upstream license is in QEV-LICENSE.txt. The pinned dependency retains its
own LICENSE and NOTICE files.

Compact runtime additionally supports the same inference sequence at upstream
revision e091f7ae8c6f064860d59be560faa27f0387ff35, with a verified bundled
ken-jo/QEV-0.5B backbone and its own distilled/calibrated heads.
